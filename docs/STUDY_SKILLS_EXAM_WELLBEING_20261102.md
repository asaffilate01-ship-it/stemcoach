# STEMCoach: study techniques, wellbeing and exam preparation (2 November 2026)

## What this phase delivers

A public, six-category **Study & Exam Skills Hub** in `/study-skills`, with **18 original, step-by-step guides** in English, German and French: planning/focus, memory/retrieval, understanding difficult topics, exam preparation, sleep/body/rest and pressure/support. Practical examples are advice, not promised results or medical treatment.

Students can select their current need, filter/search guides, follow each technique with three concrete steps, use an optional 10/20/25 minute focus timer, and tick a session-only checklist for studying, the night before or exam day. A clear wellbeing boundary encourages discussing persistent stress with trusted people and professional services. Every guide links to a relevant external guidance page and the page lists full sources.

The hub is linked from the primary app menu, signed-in student dashboard and the Q&A Clinic's local revision progress section. No login is required to read the hub or start Q&A self-checks. The account-only planner is still protected by its existing authentication route.

## Sources and editorial boundary

Original guidance was drafted with reference to:
- Education Endowment Foundation (2025), **Metacognition and Self-Regulated Learning** — https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/guidance-reports/metacognition
- NHS, **Tips on preparing for exams** — https://www.nhs.uk/mental-health/children-and-young-adults/help-for-teenagers-young-adults-and-students/tips-on-preparing-for-exams/
- Ofqual (updated 2025), **Coping with exam pressure** — https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/coping-with-exam-pressure-a-guide-for-students
- AQA, **How to manage exam stress** — https://www.aqa.org.uk/student-and-parent-support/managing-stress

Guides are not certified curriculum materials, therapy or a substitute for qualified assessment. They avoid storing health information, user stress selections, timer sessions, checklist completion and typed answers. Local translations should receive a human linguistic/educational review before external certification claims.
