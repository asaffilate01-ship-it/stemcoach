# StemCoach: PRODUCT.md

Primary users: GCSE/IGCSE, A-level and related STEM learners and their educators.

Problem solved: Accurate practice questions, adaptive quizzes, tutoring support and clear exam-preparation feedback.

Usability priorities: A question, notation, options, selection, submit state and explanation must remain readable without colour dependence. Support maths rendering, keyboard option selection, answer corrections, item provenance, difficulty and progress visibility without distracting animations. Never call unverified AI explanations authoritative.

This brief is a design aid. Verify all implementation and business claims against the repository's code, test evidence, product owners and deployed systems.
